FROSTED FACES FOUNDATION — BRAND & CONTENT KIT
Context for the SEO / AEO / GEO generation agent

This file gives the generation agent shared brand context so output is consistent,
on-voice, and citable across every dog. It supplements the agent's hard rules — it
never overrides them. In particular, the rule "use ONLY facts present in the entry"
still governs all per-dog claims. The only facts the agent may add that are not on a
dog entry are the org-level facts in Section 1, because they are true for every dog
Frosted Faces lists.

How to use: load this as the agent's context/knowledge in Agent OS, or paste it
directly above the ROLE section of the SEO/AEO/GEO prompt as a BRAND CONTEXT block.


1. WHO WE ARE (org-level facts — true for every entry)

- Name: Frosted Faces Foundation. Short brand token for titles/UI: "Frosted Faces."
- What we do: a nonprofit rescue that takes in senior and special-needs dogs and
  rehomes them into loving homes.
- Why "Frosted Faces": the greying, "frosted" muzzles of senior dogs.
- Verified mission language (from the site): "Frosted Faces Foundation rescues and
  rehomes senior dogs ... give a frosted face a warm home."
- Adoption model: foster-based — dogs live in foster homes; an individual dog's
  foster city may be shown per entry (foster_location).

- Founded: [FILL IN: year]
- Adoption fee policy: varies per dog; many are fee-free ($0). Fees, where charged,

These org facts MAY appear in ai_summary, meta_description, and structured data

2. VOICE & TONE

- Warm, dignified, factual. Senior-dog-positive: age is a feature, not a flaw.
- Honest, not saccharine. We state hard histories plainly when the entry includes
  them; we never sensationalize or milk a sad backstory for pity.
- Celebrate golden years, second chances, calm companionship, and the joy of
  adopting an older dog.
- Tone split for this task: the brand warmth lives on the website. The structured
  SEO/AEO/GEO output stays neutral and factual — answer engines cite facts and
  ignore promotional padding. One soft adopt cue in meta_description is the only
  flourish allowed.

Avoid: "damaged," "old/useless," "last chance" hype, fake urgency, pity language,
and any "perfect dog / guaranteed great with X" promises.


3. TERMINOLOGY

Prefer: senior dog, golden years, frosted face, adopt, foster, rescue, rehome,
special-needs, companion, adopter / family.

Avoid -> use instead:
- "owner" -> adopter / family
- "buy / purchase" -> adopt
- "pound" -> shelter
- "put down / euthanized" -> only reference euthanasia risk if the entry states it
  (e.g. a dog pulled from a shelter); phrase as "was at risk of euthanasia."
- Mixed breeds -> "{breed} mix"; never guess or upgrade a breed.


4. AUDIENCE & WHAT ADOPTERS ACTUALLY ASK
(This fuels AEO FAQs and keyword selection — only answer what the entry supports.)

- Who adopts: people open to calmer, lower-energy dogs — retirees, families wanting
  a mellow companion, and hospice / "fospice" adopters for dogs with limited time.
- Top questions (map each to an entry field; skip any the entry marks "unknown"):
  - Is the dog good with kids / dogs / cats?
  - Is the dog house-trained?
  - Does the dog have medical or special needs, and what ongoing care is involved?
  - What's the energy level / how much exercise?
  - What's the adoption fee, and what does it cover?
  - Where is the dog located / can I meet them?
  - How old is the dog?
  - Is this a bonded pair that must be adopted together? (some entries are)


5. SEO / AEO / GEO SPECIFICS FOR THIS BRAND

- Title brand token: "Frosted Faces" (never the full legal name — it overflows the
  60-char title limit).
- Geographic keywords: use the dog's foster_location only when it's a real
  city/region; if it's blank or equals the org name, lean on "senior dog adoption /
  rescue" + breed instead.
- Themes to surface when the entry supports them: senior dog adoption, special-needs
  / medical care, bonded pairs, calm / low-energy companions, hospice fostering.
- ai_summary anchor: always ground the dog to the org, e.g. "{Name} is available for
  adoption from Frosted Faces Foundation" (+ city if real), then the most cite-worthy
  facts.


6. BRAND-SPECIFIC GUARDRAILS (reinforce the agent rules)

- Senior is not every dog. We are a senior rescue, but not every listing is a senior
  (e.g. an age_category: adult dog). Never call a dog "senior" unless its age /
  age_category supports it. Let the org be the senior specialist; let each dog be
  exactly its stated age.
- Never invent per-dog facts — temperament, medical, location, fee. The org facts in
  Section 1 are the only shared facts you may add.
- $0 fee -> "fee-free" / "helps cover care," never "$0."
- Adopted -> past-tense the availability, drop adopt/fee CTAs and FAQs, set
  no_index: true.
